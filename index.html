Hey, I'm Mostakim Billah, an Urban and Regional Planning student at Jahangirnagar University in Dhaka and a Creative Multimedia Specialist with over three years of experience in documentary storytelling, high-retention video editing, motion graphics, and visual branding. Documentary

FEATURED FILM & LINKEDIN DISPATCH · 05:06 · ZERO TO MOTION - বাংলা

The Presence of Mughal Influence in Old Dhaka

Watch on YouTube ↗ View LinkedIn Profile ↗
OLD DHAKA, BANGLADESH PREMIERE PRO · DAVINCI RESOLVE 20 · SOUND DESIGN
Mostakim Billah Director's Note
BURP Student @ Jahangirnagar University · Video Editor & Motion Designer

Walk down any narrow lane in Old Dhaka today and four centuries of urban history still breathe beneath the chaos. When Dhaka became the Mughal capital of Bengal (Jahangirnagar) in 1610 along the Buriganga River, it didn't just inherit monuments; it inherited an entire spatial grammar.

In this short documentary created for Zero To Motion (বাংলা), I set out to trace how Mughal architecture and urbanism, from the axial gardens and cusped arches of Lalbagh Fort, Bara Katra, and historic riverfront mosques to the intimate neighborhood mahallas and living craft traditions, continue to anchor Dhaka's cultural identity amid rapid unplanned densification.

As an Urban & Regional Planning student and visual storyteller, filming and grading this piece reminded me why archaeological heritage is never just about preserving brick and lime plaster, because it is also about safeguarding collective civic memory.
