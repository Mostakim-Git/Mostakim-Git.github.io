# Mostakim Billah | Portfolio

Personal portfolio of **Mostakim Billah**, Urban & Regional Planning student at **Jahangirnagar University**, Creative Multimedia Specialist, Documentary Filmmaker, and AI Creative Technologist based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

## Highlights

- **Editorial Kinetic Design**: Smooth inertia scrolling (`Lenis`), `GSAP + ScrollTrigger` pinned visual showcase, spotlight glassmorphic navigation, 3D hero typography reveal, interactive accordions, and SVG-filtered morphing footer typography.
- **Featured Documentary Showcase**: Inline cinema player & director's field dispatch for *The Presence of Mughal Influence in Old Dhaka* (`Zero To Motion - বাংলা`).
- **Selected Software & Creative Work**:
  - **The Presence of Mughal Influence in Old Dhaka**: Urban Heritage Documentary Film ([YouTube](https://youtu.be/-eOgfXAkdDY))
  - **Jolabaddho? (জলাবদ্ধ?)**: AI-Assisted Civic Waterlogging Reporting App (`React 19`, `Gemini Vision`, `Open-Meteo`, `Capacitor`)
  - **AUDIO-rec**: Bit-Perfect UAC1/UAC2 USB Audio Workstation for Android (`Java`, `Android SDK`, `575 KB Zero-Gradle APK`)
  - **CaCa (ক্যাকা)**: Offline-First University Class Calendar & Native Java Widgets (`React 18`, `TypeScript`, `Capacitor 6`)
  - **Post-Production & Brand Identity Archive**: 3+ years across Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve 20, Photoshop & Illustrator.
